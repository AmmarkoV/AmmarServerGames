    Click a territory to select it. Drag its yellow corners or its label anchor. Shift+click near an edge to add a corner, right-click a corner to remove it. Corners snap to other territories' corners (hold Alt to stop snapping). Drag empty space to pan, scroll to zoom.

    Ports follow the sea-lines (Connections tab). Renaming also updates cards that name the territory.
